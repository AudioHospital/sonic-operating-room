## THE EXPERIENCE

When you drop a track into the Sonic Operating Room, the system analyzes the mix in real time and places every frequency range as a living object inside a 3D space:

| Element | Visual Representation |
|---|---|
| **Kick / Sub** | Pulsating icosahedron at center floor — scales with sub energy |
| **Bass** | Translucent breathing sphere — expands with low-end pressure |
| **Snare** | Metallic octahedron — flashes and spins on transients |
| **Hi-Hats** | Gold spark cluster — flickers with air frequencies |
| **Vocals** | Holographic plane — width mapped to stereo field |
| **Pads** | Atmospheric fog clouds — materializes on mid energy |
| **Melody** | Gold torus ribbons — spins with high frequency content |

**Stereo panning is physical.** Objects move left and right in 3D space based on your mix's L/R balance.  
**Phase issues trigger visual glitching.** The room destabilizes when correlation drops.  
**Mix health changes the atmosphere.** Muddy mix = room goes red. Clean mix = gold and stable.
